PUBLIC RELEASE — GHANA SITE PLAN PLOTTER

I am pleased to share Ghana Site Plan Plotter, a surveying and site-plan support tool I developed to make coordinate-based plotting and site-plan processing easier.

The tool supports multiple coordinate points, area and perimeter calculations, bearings and distances, CSV/Excel export, DXF export, KML/Google Earth export, and online satellite/map checking.
