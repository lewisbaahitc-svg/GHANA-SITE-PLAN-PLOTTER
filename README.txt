WHAT IS NEW IN V6
- Camera feature removed.
- Supports 3 to 100 points.
- CSV import/export for Excel.
- DXF export for AutoCAD.
- KML export for Google Earth.
- Online satellite/map checking. V6 provides Esri satellite imagery as the primary satellite layer, plus Google Satellite and OpenStreetMap Street Map options.
- Developer name and copyright notice added to the application and report.

HOW TO USE
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Select the coordinate system.
4. Enter the number of points (3–100) and click UPDATE POINTS.
5. Enter coordinates and click PLOT & COMPUTE.
6. Click SATELLITE / MAP when internet is available.
7. Use CSV, DXF or KML export as needed.

IMPORTANT
The online map requires internet. Satellite imagery is provided by third-party map services and is subject to their terms. The Ghana War Office conversion is a prototype/common transformation and must be checked against approved survey control for official cadastral work.

This tool is intended as a surveying/planning aid and does not replace professional survey procedures or statutory approval.
